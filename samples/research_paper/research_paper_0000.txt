Abstract. In this paper we propose a graph neural network for anomaly detection. Experiments on LibriSpeech show that our approach outperforms strong baselines.
1 Introduction. Anomaly detection remains a challenging problem. Prior work has largely relied on hand-crafted features; we instead learn representations end to end.
2 Related work. Earlier studies [1], [2] explored a graph neural network. Our work differs in its training objective and evaluation protocol.
3 Method. Let x denote the input and y the label. We minimise the cross-entropy loss with weight decay 1e-4 using the Adam optimiser.
